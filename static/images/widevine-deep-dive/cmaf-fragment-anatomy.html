CMAF Fragment, Chunk and CENC Anatomy

One CMAF track combines a stable header with addressable segments, independently decodable fragments and progressively publishable chunks

Fragment Is the Switching Unit

  • Alternative tracks align fragment boundaries on a common timeline
  • A fragment starts at an independently decodable random access point
  • A segment is an addressable wrapper containing one or more fragments

Chunk Is the Publication Unit

  • A chunk carries a sequential subset of one fragment's samples
  • Completed chunks can be published before the parent segment is complete
  • Later chunks may depend on samples from the same fragment

CENC Spans Header and Chunk

  • pssh and tenc establish DRM initialization and track defaults
  • seig and senc-family boxes describe effective per-sample protection
  • mdat remains encrypted media; the License and raw key stay out of band
